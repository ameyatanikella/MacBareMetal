2. BUFFER SLOTS
  add  (a[id] + b[id]) vs rsub (b[id] - a[id]) share their two load instructions;
  the loads differ only at: byte 5 (00 vs 04), byte 13 (04 vs 00)
  copy_a and copy_b compile to identical code: True
  so the byte after 0f/1f/2f/3f selects among bindings in steps of 4, numbered
  by order of use, and which Metal buffer that is must be recorded outside the code.
